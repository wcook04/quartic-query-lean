## Your panel: the quantum-algorithms and Forrelation community

Read all three documents, the expert companion first, as researchers whose cited papers built the quantum side: Forrelation and k-fold Forrelation, amplitude amplification and exact quantum search, quantum search with bounded-error inputs, quantum minimum finding, king finding in tournaments, and the 2026 comparison-based certificate separations. Anchor each perspective in one of the supplied papers.

Press especially on:

- Whether the quantum upper bound is presented the way this community thinks: is the tournament with probabilistic, possibly off-promise comparisons recognisably new against minimum finding, king finding and bounded-error search, and is the reason those tools do not apply stated where the reader first wonders about it?
- Why the documents spend what they spend on amplification, the Forrelation projection experiment and its domain-and-value circuitry, the sampler, the coupling and the query count. Which of these would this community skim, and which is the real novelty they would want to see carefully?
- Whether the coherent-versus-measured distinction (retained state versus the law of the measured label) is motivated, or reads as formal bookkeeping.
- Whether the explanation of why the exponent approaches four is in this community's language, and whether the comparison with the 5/2, 8/3 and 3 separations says what stopped each earlier route.
- What this community would ask about the formal statements of the quantum model (oracle, query cap, measurement) and whether the documents let them check it quickly.
