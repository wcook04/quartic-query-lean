## Your panel: readers rebuilding the complete argument

The lead is the **reasoning record**. Use finding prefix `RR10-`. The primary
chronological pass covers Sections 1–7, every appendix, claim catalogue,
formal-statement discussion and reference, in publication order. Its length is
not permission to review only the introduction or navigation. Reading routes
are content to test, not a waiver of this assignment. Declare incomplete ranges
honestly if a complete pass cannot be finished.

Use four simulated perspectives, with the scope of each specialist reread stated:

1. A quantum-algorithms researcher reconstructing the circuit, sampler,
   amplification, comparison tournament and final verification.
2. A randomized lower-bound researcher reconstructing the hard law, direct
   product, capped predictor and uniform Forrelation argument.
3. A mathematician outside query complexity attempting the complete proof and
   seeking the reason each auxiliary lemma is introduced.
4. A formal-methods reader comparing mathematical claims with Lean statements
   and distinguishing the printed derivation, formal implementation, transport
   arguments and historical execution evidence.

A long record can be exhaustive and still fail to make its argument intelligible.
Ask whether a reader can keep the central proof in view while descending into
technical detail and can recover it afterward. Inspect how local calculations
connect to global objectives, how assumptions survive changes of notation and
how variant parameter regimes are distinguished. Reconstruct the actual steps;
do not replace that work with a navigation audit or a check of recorded labels.

After the freeze, compare with substantial mathematical treatments that manage
similar demands. Look for useful choices in lemma placement, proof previews,
worked calculations, local summaries and the separation of current reasoning
from historical discovery. Do not import another genre's brevity at the expense
of this record's reconstructibility.

Consider structural transformation where it could materially help: consolidating
repeated explanations, moving historical remarks, introducing an interface before
its construction, reorganizing appendices or changing the route through a proof.
A proposal must identify affected passages and dependencies, preserve the actual
mathematics and explain how the full argument remains recoverable. Wholesale
shortening is not the goal; neither is preserving the present architecture by
default.

Check catalogue and formal-source destinations for the specific claims they
promise. Assess whether evidence appendices let the reader find the current
status before historical detail. Keep source presence, statement agreement and
recorded execution distinct. Review the writing and mathematical account; do not
turn this assignment into another formal build or a demand for stronger theorems.
