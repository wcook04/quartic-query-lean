# Round-ten packet selection

Three packets each assign one complete lead-paper review: the
[expert companion](expert_paper.md), [exposition](exposition.md) and
[reasoning record](reasoning_record.md). The [common request](common.md),
[chronological protocol](chronological_reading.md) and [boundaries](boundaries.md)
govern them. The aim is exceptionally well-written mathematics by the standards
of the relevant literature, with concrete improvements at sentence, argument and
whole-paper scales. There are no finding quotas or praise-only entries.

The reviewer records and freezes a prospective first pass before consulting
historical reviews, other accounts or writing guidance. The
[literature standard](literature_standard.md) is a second-pass resource. Actual
coverage, prior exposure, detours and retrospective annotations must remain
visible; neither a large finding count nor a well-formed log proves a cold read.

Each packet carries all three revised manuscripts and source-bound PDFs,
numbered reading copies, the full 42-source existing literature annex, the merged
writing method, register measurements and exemplars. Each also includes complete
PDFs and extracted text for the six sources used by the literature standard and
supplementary mathematical editions. The generated packet-root
`LITERATURE_COVERAGE.json` maps citations to the supplied editions and remaining
gaps; `SUPPLEMENTARY_LITERATURE.json` identifies the added files. Yao (1977) and
Nisan (1991) remain inaccessible in full text. The cited Barnum–Saks–Szegedy
2003 edition is unavailable; the earlier 2002 primary version is supplied with
that distinction explicit. Full-context availability is not a claim of complete
reading or exact-edition coverage. Open these resources after the first-pass
freeze. All three round-nine returns, their original
archives and complete preserved members, and the round-nine individual
dispositions and revision evidence are included. They can be challenged with
current evidence; their instructions do not override this request. Earlier
rounds remain historical context.

[Production notes](BUILD.md) describe source selection and validation boundaries.
The [round-ten builder](../../../tools/build_round10_packets.py) owns packet
assembly. Generated batch audits identify the frozen inputs and delivered ZIP
hashes. Building a packet does not send it, establish a review or validate a proof.
