## Your panel: specialist referees and an exacting editor

The lead is the **expert companion**, reviewed as a submission to a strong theory
venue by readers who know query complexity but have not read OpenAI's preprint.
Use finding prefix `XP10-`. Complete its primary chronological pass before opening
the supporting record passages or literature.

Use three simulated perspectives, one at a time, and state their actual coverage:

1. A quantum-algorithms researcher reconstructing the comparison, weighted
   tournament, state preparation, amplification and final verification.
2. A randomized lower-bound researcher reconstructing the planted law, erasure,
   simulator and direct-product argument, including parameter dependence.
3. An editor assessing whether the mathematical question, contribution, credit
   and proof design emerge with the economy and force expected of a strong paper.

After the freeze, anchor these backgrounds in the supplied primary papers and
seek relevant treatments beyond them. Compare how those sources introduce a
construction, state an intermediate guarantee and turn technical accounting into
a legible argument. Name the passage and the transferable choice, not merely an
author whose prose you admire.

Examine whether the first page establishes what is proved and what this account
contributes; whether the construction's ingredients arrive with their purpose;
and whether each principal reduction can be reconstructed at the level promised
by the paper. Inspect the record destinations after the first pass: a valid link
must support the particular job delegated to it, not just contain related words.

Ask where the paper should be more economical and where its compression forces
an expert to supply a substantial missing argument. Consider a different order
of lemmas or a cleaner separation of interfaces from implementations if it would
make the proof design visible. Evaluate the counterexamples, parameter refinement
and formal-statement discussion by their contribution to this reading task.
Keep fixed-parameter claims, uniform refinements and recorded formal evidence
within their stated scopes; accurate scope need not become intrusive bookkeeping.

A referee report should identify mathematical faults when substantiated and
editorial opportunities even when the mathematics is sound. Distinguish those
judgments. The desired next edition is an excellent specialist account, not a
longer catalogue of qualifications or a miniature copy of the reasoning record.
