# Round-seven packet selection

Four feedback panels on the round-six revisions of the three documents, asking for comprehensive complaints and advice rather than located corrections: newcomers reading the exposition; the quantum-algorithms community; the randomized lower-bound and separations community; and editors and referees following the funnel from exposition to record to Lean. Every packet carries the three documents and their PDFs, the long record's link closure, the preprint, a sample of the literature chosen per panel with an instruction to search online beyond it, the full writing method (all skills and the register stage with its rules, literature profile and draft measurements), the reader principles, the exemplar papers, and the round-six dispositions, plans and fidelity review.
