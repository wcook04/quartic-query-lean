# Round-eight packet selection

Two feedback packets on the round-seven revisions, one per short paper. The expert-paper packet asks specialists in quantum algorithms, randomized lower bounds and separations to referee the expert companion as a research paper. The exposition packet asks newcomers and teachers to test whether the exposition teaches the theorem to a mathematically literate reader outside the field. Each packet carries all three documents and their PDFs, so the reader can follow links into the long record. It also carries the record's link closure, the preprint, and a sample of the literature chosen for its panel, with an instruction to search online beyond it. The rest of the packet is the full writing method (all skills and the register stage with its rules, literature profile and draft measurements), the reader principles, the exemplar papers, and the round-seven synthesis, revision notes, audits and verdicts.
