## Your panel: referees of the expert companion

The lead paper is the **expert companion**. Referee it as a submission to a strong theory venue, read by specialists who know query complexity but have not read OpenAI's preprint. Read it in full, then the record sections it links to for the two principal reductions.

Read as three referees, one at a time, and keep their reactions separate:

1. a quantum-algorithms researcher whose background is the cited work on amplitude amplification, quantum search with bounded-error inputs, minimum finding and Forrelation;
2. a randomized lower-bound researcher whose background is the cited work on cheat sheets, pointer functions, Forrelation lower bounds, direct product theorems and the earlier separations;
3. a handling editor who cares above all whether the paper says what is new, credits what is not, and reads well from the first sentence.

Anchor each referee in the supplied papers, and search online for the newest related work and for how the field's best papers present constructions of this kind.

Press especially on:

- The first page: does a specialist know within a paragraph what is proved, what is reconstructed from OpenAI and what this paper adds? Is the result stated precisely, at the point its definitions support it?
- The construction: is each ingredient (Forrelation promise, addressed cells, transcripts, pointers) introduced at the moment it is needed, with the reason it is needed?
- The quantum upper bound: the comparison, the weighted tournament, its sampling and the final verification. Would a referee from the quantum side accept each step as written, and see why the obvious alternatives fail?
- The classical lower bound: the planted distribution, the erased zero, the capped simulator and the direct-product step. Would a referee from the lower-bound side accept the accounting, and see where the exponent is kept?
- Credit: is every predecessor credited accurately and proportionately, and checked against the supplied papers?
- Weighting: what a specialist would skip, what they would want expanded, and whether Appendix A's counterexamples and the formal-statement section earn their places.
- Prose against the supplied papers, sentence by sentence.
