# Round-nine packet selection

Three packets, one lead paper each: [expert companion](expert_paper.md),
[exposition](exposition.md), and [reasoning record](reasoning_record.md).
The [common request](common.md), [sequential protocol](chronological_reading.md)
and [boundaries](boundaries.md) govern all three. They request improvement-only
findings and a sentence-by-sentence first-encounter audit before retrospective
verification. A large count does not establish coverage; unreviewed ranges must
be declared. The long-record packet covers the full document and appendices.

Every packet carries all three current manuscripts, fresh source-bound PDFs,
line-numbered reading copies, the preprint, relevant literature, the complete
writing method, current register measurements, reader principles and exemplars.
Both round-eight returns and the revision/adjudication records are supplied in
full as text/JSON, with the round-seven audits retained as earlier history.
Historical instructions do not override the current review request. Native
render receipts reject stale PDFs; inspection remains a separate requirement.
The [builder](../../../tools/build_round9_packets.py) preserves prior batches
and performs the independent ZIP and private-name checks. Nothing is sent.

[Production notes](BUILD.md) identify the source editions, method selection and
validation scope. The generated batch audit records the delivered ZIP hashes.
