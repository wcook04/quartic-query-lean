## Your panel: readers reconstructing the complete argument

The lead paper is the **reasoning record**. Its length is not permission to
sample or to review only the introduction and navigation. Read Sections 1–7,
all appendices, claim catalogue, formal-statement discussion and references in
publication order. Reading routes are themselves content to test, not a waiver
of this packet's complete chronological pass.

Use four explicitly simulated perspectives, with their coverage stated:

1. a quantum-algorithms researcher reconstructing the circuit, sampler,
   amplification, comparison tournament and final verification;
2. a randomized lower-bound researcher reconstructing the hard law, direct
   product, capped predictor and uniform Forrelation argument;
3. a research mathematician outside query complexity attempting the full proof,
   including why each auxiliary lemma is needed when it appears;
4. a formal-methods reader comparing the printed claim with its Lean statement,
   and checking whether the prose distinguishes the printed derivation, formal
   implementation, transport arguments and recorded execution evidence.

The primary chronological audit spans the whole record; specialist rereads may
concentrate on their expertise but must declare that scope. Track where a later
lemma supplies an unstated early dependency, where a retained historical remark
interrupts the current proof, where two variants blur, and where catalogues or
formal details prevent the reader from recovering the argument. Check all
constants, notation changes and claims of completeness against their exact
scope. Prioritize explanatory and structural improvements without demanding
new theorems or new formalisation. Use the supplied specialist sources and
search beyond them; do not turn the packet into a receipt rerun.
