## Your job: the randomized lower bound in both papers

Read the randomized lower bound in the expert companion and in the exposition, and the corresponding sections of the preprint and the long record. Read as the authors of direct product theorems for randomized query complexity, of Yao's minimax principle and of the Forrelation lower bounds would read it.

Judge in particular:

- **The planted input.** Is the hard distribution introduced with its purpose visible? Is the planted pair and the first-difference argument presented so that an expert sees at once why a classical algorithm must pay for the address or the hidden zero?
- **The erased zero.** Is the identity Pr[erased zero is queried] = E[Z]/m stated, and is it clear that it needs the planted index to be independent of the whole run? Is there a counterexample showing what fails otherwise?
- **The capped direct product.** Are the separate caps, joint independence of coins and side information, and the joint event (success and within cap) rather than conditional success stated precisely? Is the trap of conditioning on cheap runs shown with a small example? Is the relation to Drucker's theorem and Shaltiel's counterexamples stated exactly, with locators, including what is borrowed and what is proved here?
- **Minimax and averaging.** Is the use of Yao's principle stated where the distributional bound becomes a randomized bound, without re-proving what experts know?
- **Constants and quantifiers.** Are the constants traceable, and are fixed k, the threshold and the growing-k remark kept separate?
- **Placement in the literature.** Use Drucker, Shaltiel, the cheat-sheet and pointer-function lower bounds, and the Forrelation lower bounds of Bansal–Sinha, Sherstov–Storozhenko–Wu and Girish–Servedio to judge what the experts expect to be cited and what they expect to be argued.
- **Teaching versus refereeing.** Do the exposition's AND example and planted-pair figure teach the joint-versus-conditional distinction to a student, and does the companion avoid re-teaching it to experts?
