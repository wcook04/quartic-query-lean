## Boundaries

- Improve the writing of what exists. Do not expand the scope, propose new theorems, new constructions, stronger bounds or new formalisation work. A finding may say that an existing argument is missing from a paper, out of place or unclear; it may not ask for mathematics the sources do not contain.
- Do not rewrite. No replacement sections, no rewritten abstract, no new manuscript.
- Keep facts separate: a source being present, a Lean build passing, Comparator accepting a target and a human reviewing the mathematics are four different facts. Do not claim to have compiled Lean, run Comparator or rendered a PDF unless you actually did, and say where.
- Quote the current drafts exactly. A finding whose quotation does not occur in the current Markdown is unusable.
- The exemplars concern another theorem and are guidance on structure only. The literature supplies authority for credit and for how the field writes; it is not evidence about this construction beyond what it states.
- When a cited source is listed as cited only, use its identifier and locator; do not claim to have read it.
- Return advice only. The authors decide, revise and check. Do not claim to have changed any file.
