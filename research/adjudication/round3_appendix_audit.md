Each maps to exactly one current corpus ID. The [machine-readable audit](round3_appendix_audit.json) preserves, for every entry, the original quoted text, historical evidence fields and declaration ties, the current canonical text and hashes, the earlier generated corpus snapshot, and the new backend's in-memory evidence and declaration-level details. It also records the complete local Lean/configuration snapshot that those in-memory results used. The preserved baseline remains unchanged.

Eleven canonical statement texts differ from their original ledger versions: `cor-cubic-refuted`, `eq-5`, `claim-bs-to-L`, `eq-8`, `def-win-at-address`, `lem-2.2.i`, `lem-3.1`, `claim-minimax-mu`, `eq-17`, `claim-numeric`, and `def-deg`. ## Evidence freshness

At this audit's specific source snapshot, the new [corpus builder](../../tools/build_corpus.py), invoked in memory without writing generated files, reported 121 `legacy_unbound` statement reviews and 121 effective `unassessed` relations. It reported statement-level build and comparison as `not_run` and dependency reach as `stale` for all 121. **This does not mean no builds or comparisons were ever run**: individual ties preserve historical receipts and may classify them as `stale` against the complete present local source/configuration set. In particular, new Lean files added concurrently invalidate a whole-source receipt without proving a theorem false. Relation review, Lean build, Comparator acceptance and headline dependency reach are independent facts.

A review binding must follow actual assessment of the complete statement, definitions, theorem type and assumptions. New build or comparison receipts do not themselves refresh a semantic review. The prior `corpus/statements.json` values in this audit were generated under the earlier policy, so they must not be presented as current review or operation status. Re-run the builder and validate after the proof and receipt work ends; the recorded in-memory snapshot can itself become stale. Follow the [statement evidence contract](../../docs/statement_evidence_contract.md), including its deliberately conservative binding to all local Lean/configuration bytes and its limits on external dependency and executable identity.

## Corrections requiring explicit reader-facing treatment

- **`claim-huang-spectrum`:** the frozen statement and the present canonical statement omit the `d ≥ 1` qualification needed for two distinct eigenvalues `±√d` with multiplicities `2^(d−1)`. At `d = 0`, the matrix is `[0]`; the square and trace identities still hold. This audit records a proposed corrected statement; it does not silently rewrite the canonical ledger. See [the foundation adjudication](round3_foundations.md).
- **`claim-bs-to-L`:** the corrected canonical wording differs from the frozen original; the JSON carries both and their hashes. Preserve that provenance. The separate assessment of a displayed comparison in the Bansal–Sinha proof is a source-specific argument and does not disprove their theorem. See [the independent source reading](bansal_sinha_section54_second_reading.md).
- **`lem-3.1`:** the abstract good-state result and physical label-law implementations have different types. The designated original-output-subsystem bridge remains a separate formal fidelity target; the measured-label proof of the upper bound does not require it. The product/Bell counterexample refutes an inference from label law to state, not physical implementability.
- **`prop-3.2`:** the current source contains a physical list-valued tournament theorem. The older account treating the list interface as absent is superseded at source level; its review, compilation and comparison remain declaration- and snapshot-specific.
- **`eq-19` and `claim-EZ`:** the former's literal equality-and-inequality display is not established merely by an inequality-only tie. New whole-display declarations must be individually built, reviewed and compared before their own evidence is credited. The separate `claim-EZ` early/late route is not discharged by a conjunction wrapper; the existing stronger endpoint uses an alternative finite-world induction.
- **`lem-4.1`, `claim-address-guess`, `eq-16`:** finite mixtures and jointly independent arbitrary side information are distinct formal interfaces. A finite-behavior pushforward is the missing transfer for the latter; the queried and unchanged factors of `eq-16` are separate formal components. The joint-event version for an uncapped adaptive tree also needs a truncation theorem.
- **`eq-18`, `eq-23`, `claim-tail`:** absolute-value discrepancy bounds are stronger than the preprint's one-sided versions. Exact logarithmic-envelope and convergence clauses must be attributed to their respective declarations, rather than inferred from a neighboring bound.
- **Construction rows:** the semantic/output-test equivalence carries the realizing-circuit premise. Pointer depth is distinct from the number of essential physical coordinates. A clean XOR oracle and a single specialization coherent over both candidates and tests are additional interfaces, not automatically present in the fixed-pair evaluator.

The source-level and proof-route distinctions for the last four points are detailed in [the construction adjudication](round3_construction.md). This audit does not claim new Lean or Comparator execution, human review, final paper acceptance, or automatic migration of historical statement judgments. The corrected spectrum statement and the displayed-comparison assessment remain separately source-bound decisions for their owners.
