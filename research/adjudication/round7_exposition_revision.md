# Round 7 exposition revision

## Scope and decision

This revision owns `docs/exposition.md`. It explains the established total-function separation to readers with finite-dimensional linear algebra, elementary probability and asymptotic notation, including readers without quantum-query training. Its endpoint is operational: trace a certificate, reconstruct the pivot distribution, and explain the capped predictor in the planted lower bound. It changes no Lean theorem and asserts no new mathematical result. OpenAI retains credit for the theorem and construction. The expert companion and reasoning record remain separate owners.

The revision reads the four panels as advisory findings, not authority to strengthen claims. The newcomer feedback, reading logs and literature comparisons diagnose first-use gaps; the quantum and classical checks adjudicate the mathematical changes. Successful examples survive: signed Forrelation, the false domain certificate, dominant nonwinner, cyclic losers, independent planted-column counterexample, cap-event warning and order of limits.

Plan executed: (1) teach the missing query and quantum operations; (2) state the two costs and one mechanism before detailed history; (3) make local verification executable with an actual circuit gate; (4) put sampling examples before amplification and retain only the amplification contract plus its invariant; (5) show the capped simulator and its event inclusion on the existing XOR example; (6) repair exact quantifiers, probability scope, costs and formal correspondence; (7) compare the register and inspect the native render.

## Mathematical changes adjudicated before the prose pass

- **Caps:** stopping and random iteration counts change realized counts. Only input-independent upper caps are asserted. The amplifier is stated for `0<gamma<=1/2`, `0<eta<1/2`, with its zero-success and below-threshold cases.
- **Fixed parameter:** `k=4` excludes every exponent below 3.5 and therefore proves the infimum is at least 3.5. `k=5` excludes the endpoint inequality with exponent 3.5 by the displayed strict-power argument. The analogous cubic sentence is corrected.
- **Toy hardness:** `L_{2,4}=0` does not deny existence of a hard zero-query law. It makes the positive-budget asymptotic use unavailable at this size.
- **Residual zero:** condition on the full prefix before a fresh query and before first discovery, with distinct positions counted; repeat queries produce no new discovery.
- **Verification:** a failed test is deterministic data, but the search for one has bounded error. The exact pivot-amplification calculation in Answer 2 does not imply certain rejection of losers.
- **Sampler:** the abstract matrix has `a23=1/3`, `a24=3/4`, `a34=2/5`, reverse probabilities summing to one, and candidate 1 dominant. After pivot 2 the weights are `(1,0,2/3,1/4)`, preparation success `23/48`, conditional labels `(12/23,0,8/23,3/23)`. This is explicitly an abstract comparison example, not an asserted board realization.
- **Simulator:** the imported XOR trace has a light true-cell event of probability `1/8` and total predictor success `1/4`; default successes legitimately overcount. Question 3b changes the query order and preserves the whole-run meaning of lightness.
- **Floor constants:** deleted the insufficient single `t>=8` justification for both floors. Both quantities tend to infinity for fixed `k`; the same `1/400` bound follows eventually, together with the separate Forrelation threshold.

The quantum and classical audit agents checked these mathematical passages after revision. The quantum audit independently evaluated all 256 inputs of the supplied circuit JSON with its own evaluator, including the actual new gate trace. No returned executable was run by this writer.
