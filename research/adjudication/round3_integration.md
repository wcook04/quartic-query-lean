## Mathematical boundaries that change the next action

The fixed-parameter family already supplies the required optimal-exponent
argument. Existing named construction, circuit and ratio wrappers must be
consumed rather than requested again. Ordinary sharp query bounds and the
constants in distinct formal Appendix routes remain distinct.

The full equation-(19) statement joins the probability/expectation equality and
its bound. Its proof uses the existing finite zero-search route; it does not
thereby formalize the paper's early/late decomposition. The independent target
spells out the product law and discovery event and proves bridges to the
implementation. It still uses declared coordinate and query-tree interfaces.

Physical measured-label correctness and retained quantum-state correctness are
separate interfaces. The existing upper-bound endpoint uses the former. The
remaining retained-state bridge needs a fixed original subsystem, explicit
success flag, branchwise factorization, zero-success behavior and bounded query
cost. A finite Bell/product-state witness distinguishes the interfaces; it is
not a general impossibility result.

The finite-mixture direct-product theorem is already available. Transferring an
arbitrary jointly independent probability-space source to finite behavior, and
truncating an uncapped algorithm before its first budget excess, are separate
remaining statements. Conditioning on the budget event is not a substitute.

Bansal–Sinha's stated theorem is separated from the logarithmic comparison in
the inspected displayed proof. The correction does not declare their theorem
false. The Huang spectrum row requires positive dimension for its two distinct
eigenvalues. Source editions and adversary normalizations remain explicit.

## Evidence and publication boundary

A fresh build or comparison cannot renew an old judgment that a Lean declaration
expresses a paper statement. Authored review bindings now cover the corrected
statement row, judgment, declaration and complete local Lean/configuration
snapshot. Missing bindings remain historical; changed bindings become stale.
Reviewers explicitly renew only the ties they have inspected.

The [maintained reasoning record](../../docs/reasoning_record.md) is separate
from immutable return prose. Its bibliography, correspondence and review sidecars
belong beside it. The [next-scope recommendation](round3_next_scope.md) makes
independent review of those exact integrated bytes a prerequisite for companion
drafting packets. The next external batch is selected from remaining work;
the suggested count is not a quota.

The evidence backend and extraction are preparation for the later reader.
No website, film, public submission, human review or acceptance follows from
these local checks. The next packets must use the actual integrated record and
accepted evidence snapshot, not anticipated returns from concurrent jobs.

## Structural revision from existing long records

The operator rejected the first rendered model/evidence table. Its five narrow
columns fragmented complete explanations and declaration types despite passing
overflow and glyph checks. Mechanical render checks were not design acceptance.
The [source dossier](round3_structure_sources.md) records the actual SK and
Erdős 257 sources, revisions and visual specimens read for the repair.

The revision adapts SK's full-width mathematical transport and scope explanations,
with execution details kept separately, and 257's staged mechanism and boundary
arguments. The model contract and quantum-state correspondence are authored as
connected explanations. The printable catalogue preserves all 121 claim texts
and proof destinations; exhaustive per-declaration metadata belongs in the
correspondence sidecar. Compact numerical and case comparisons were also inspected in the actual
render. This treatment applies across the document, not only to the operator's
screenshot. A final read caught an inherited no-new-execution sentence whose
local placement obscured the newer global checks. The author clarified all eight
sibling passages while preserving the historical audits and their limits. The
successor requests now require that same attribution discipline. Successor writing requests carry the actual structural
sources and the same acceptance rule.

The complete tie audit identifies a specialized physical Forrelation program
preserving the ordinary sharp query budget as a separate obligation from general
retained-state amplification. Neither changes the asymptotic separation theorem;
the existing physical wrapper's factor-two budget remains explicit until the
sharper endpoint is proved. Both now belong to one substantial quantum-completion
job, with their proofs and consumers kept distinct.

Following the operator's request for larger, open-ended returns, the next batch
combines related formal work rather than assigning a ZIP to each narrow lemma.
Its seven outcomes are the expert and examples-first papers, quantum completion,
classical completion, whole-corpus fidelity and independent specifications,
the existing backend's publication-funnel integration, and an adversarial
mathematical/reader audit with concrete repairs. Each has a main deliverable and
room to pursue adjacent high-value solutions. The coverage job selects claims
outside the two mechanism owners; the backend job implements the existing
interfaces; no job assumes a sibling's future return. Every packet carries the
full record, role-matched sources and literature, exact Lean context, and the
relevant skills and actual SK/257 specimens. Source and linked-file completeness
are checked per packet before release. This includes non-Markdown implementation
dependencies: the renderer consumes the retained return-02 header, layout filter
and original rendering source. The backend job also needs the quartic tool tests,
not just lean-exposition's tests. A curated packet is distinct from a full custody
checkout; its read map must name the commands that its actual contents support.
