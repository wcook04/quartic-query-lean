These returns are AI-assisted advisory work, not a human referee report. They do not themselves execute Lean or Comparator.

1. **Repair the historical literature account.** [Bansal–Sinha v3 §5.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2008.07003) states its quantitative theorem but its displayed comparison at source `main.tex` 1180–1181 replaces a factor `L^((ℓ−1)/2)` by `L^(ℓ(k−1)/(2k))`, where `L=log(kN)`. Their ratio is `L^((ℓ/k−1)/2)`, unbounded for `ℓ>k`; such levels occur for `k≥3`. At `k=2` this objection does not apply. The display does not establish the stated sharper logarithmic threshold from those terms; it does **not** disprove the theorem. The repository's [independent source reading](bansal_sinha_section54_second_reading.md) and return 10's source audit give the calculation and a weaker sufficient bound. The local `ForrelationLower.bansalSinhaLower` is a distinct proof of the fixed-parameter endpoint, not a proof of the entire published quantitative theorem.
2. **Fix the canonical spectrum statement.** `claim-huang-spectrum` needs `d≥1` for two *distinct* eigenvalues `±√d`, each of multiplicity `2^(d−1)`; at `d=0` the matrix is `[0]`. The square and trace identities still make sense at zero. Apply through the canonical corpus owner and regenerate its projections; return 08 proposes the correction and independently tests dimensions 0–4.
3. **Refresh evidence by exact type.** [ConstructionFamily.lean](../../lean/QuarticQuery/Paper/ConstructionFamily.lean) contains `construction_family_named` and `construction_equation_two`; [ClassicalAsymptotics.lean](../../lean/QuarticQuery/Paper/ClassicalAsymptotics.lean) contains `classical_Fkm_ratio`; [ConstructionForrelationComplete.lean](../../lean/QuarticQuery/Paper/ConstructionForrelationComplete.lean) contains the complete quantum and conjunction wrappers; [Uniform.lean](../../lean/QuarticQuery/ForrelationLower/Uniform.lean) contains the `2/18225` uniform endpoint and explicit threshold `3499200 k²`. Obsolete claims that these are absent must be replaced, not reimplemented. A named Comparator result for one theorem does not automatically transfer to a neighboring wrapper. Bind each current build, audit, dependency and comparison to its own exact statement and current bytes.
4. **Correct the appendix constant and routes.** The [published Špalek–Szegedy theorem](https://theoryofcomputing.org/articles/v002a001/v002a001.pdf), under its displayed spectral normalization, claims a prefactor twice the standard one. Return 08's exact two-bit parity witness has one exact query and spectral ratio two, contradicting the printed prefactor even at error `1/100`. The [Høyer–Lee–Špalek paper](https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0611054) supplies the standard denominator two. This is a source-specific mathematical assessment, not a claimed author-confirmed erratum. Keep the 20736 headline dependency, 1500625 literal appendix route, and 19600 hybrid distinct. Do not merge the block-sensitive star and sensitivity graph.
5. **Preserve the quantum implementation boundary.** Return 02's ordinary `qk` phase-query presentation and the present generic `2qk` compiled cap are different resource claims. Its complete wrapper binds a common isometry, law and continuation; the proposed explicit tensor-state identity is not a new Lean theorem. Modular words represent residues; prove signed recovery at the final comparisons rather than asserting signed recovery for every register.
6. **Restore logical and model scope.** For each `α<4`, choose `k>2/(4−α)` and then hold `k` fixed while dimension grows; both bounds concern the same function. One fixed `k` does not establish the endpoint four. The classical cap covers all syntactic branches, including off-promise; success is required only on the promise, and the algorithm precedes the input quantifier. Deferred measurement preserves acceptance mass under an embedding, not necessarily the final state. Bit/phase conversion needs control or idle/reference capability.
