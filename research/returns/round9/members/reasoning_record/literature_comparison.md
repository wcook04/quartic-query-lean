## How the comparisons were used

The attached literature shelf, a search result and a read source are different evidence classes. Each item in the source inventory states what was actually opened and which sections were used. Unread items are not claimed as corroboration. Publisher metadata does not verify a proof, and an edition's theorem number must not be transferred to another edition without checking it. The review uses primary papers and official documentation for technical claims; no forum or secondary summary supplies a proof criticism.

The register measurements were consulted as detectors. No new numeric corpus baseline was constructed. The supplied profile has 42 documents and is shared across several genres, so it does not set a target for the long record's apparatus, proof prose, catalogue or pedagogical examples. A local task—recovering a conditional law, checking a cap, identifying a theorem's scope—decides whether to split, expand, move or shorten a passage. Neither word-count reduction nor movement toward a corpus median is used as evidence of clearer mathematics.

No external-source quotation is needed for the comparisons above. Source passages are paraphrased within their read scope, with exact source locations and editions retained in `feedback.json` and `checks/sources.json`. Quotations in the finding locators are from the user's supplied manuscripts and are limited to 25 words each.
