# Meaning and limits of this ledger

The source was read completely before the first-pass narrative was frozen. This is one model simulation. Inherited transcript exposure, tool-output previews and recovery rereads are disclosed in `first_pass.md` and `coverage.json`. It is not a blind human experiment.

The JSONL is a source-unit accounting ledger. Its exact source offsets, short quotation, unit digest and locators allow boundary inspection against the supplied packet. A script protects math and abbreviations and separates Markdown formats; it does not read, understand, prove, or certify sentences. The actual source reading and the script’s coverage result are different claims.

The reader states were reconstructed after the narrative freeze. The manually written passage contexts explain what was available, the local reading task, and the question being followed. Each unit carries its exact local assertion and the immediately preceding content reference. The carried question is contextual, not evidence that every intervening sentence left it unresolved. A separate contemporaneous thought log was not kept before each next sentence, so the strict prospective protocol is not satisfied. Metadata units are explicitly classified and do not masquerade as mathematical prose.

The integrated `issue_ids` refer only to accepted RR9 findings. Post-read dispositions record rejected and merged preliminary observations. The rough195 was never an accepted-finding total. Full unit text is not reprinted; each source unit is bound by offsets and SHA-256.
