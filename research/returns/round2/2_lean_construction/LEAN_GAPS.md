1. **claim-proj-prob:** the supplied exact law and generic compiler give k abstract reads and at most 2k physical bit queries. ConstructionPhase.lean proves the local one-bit-query phase operation on arbitrary entangled minus-answer states. The complete projection circuit is not packaged as a `Runs k` witness. The O_k repetition theorem in Lemma 2.1(ii) permits the existing factor of two.
2. **claim-circuit-size:** the actual Boolean circuit's final outputs and size are supplied by the repository's modular arithmetic proof. ConstructionArithmetic.lean gives polynomial magnitude bounds for the ideal integer walk, its prefixes and butterfly blocks. A theorem relating every stored modular register to an exact signed intermediate integer remains absent. No incorrect width or overflow instance has been demonstrated.
