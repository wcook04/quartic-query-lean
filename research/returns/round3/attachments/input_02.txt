### The retained state

Set
\[
\alpha_1=\Pi\psi_z,\qquad
\alpha_0=(I-\Pi)\psi_z.
\]
These vectors are orthogonal and satisfy
\[
\|\alpha_1\|^2=p,\qquad
\|\alpha_0\|^2=1-p.
\]

A query-free unitary records the projection outcome coherently in a fresh flag, producing
\[
\alpha_0\otimes|0\rangle+\alpha_1\otimes|1\rangle.
\]

For \(q\) fresh trials, after a fixed register permutation, the retained state is
\[
\sum_{h\in\{0,1\}^q}
\left(\bigotimes_{j=1}^{q}\alpha_{h_j}\right)\otimes|h\rangle.
\]

The flag strings occupy orthogonal subspaces. A string of weight \(s\) has squared norm \(p^s(1-p)^{q-s}\). Reversibly computing its weight and the threshold bit therefore gives the output law
\[
\mathcal L_z(1)
=
\sum_{s=0}^{q}
\mathbf1_{\{s\ge q\delta^2/2\}}
{q\choose s}p(z)^s(1-p(z))^{q-s},

with \(\mathcal L_z(0)=1-\mathcal L_z(1)\).

This derivation establishes the measured law of **that same preparation**, with the address, flag, and count registers retained. It also establishes a normalized Boolean output law on every input, including unpromised ones.

## 4. Modular arithmetic is sufficient for the circuit’s exact output

A major clarification concerns what the implemented registers represent.

The arithmetic constructors maintain congruences modulo \(2^w\). That invariant propagates through addition, subtraction, sign multiplication, and the Walsh recursion. Correctness of the final comparisons then requires signed-range bounds for the integers actually inspected at the end.

Put
\[
w=5k+3+t(k+1)
\]
and define
\[
A=2^{5k}N-M,\qquad
B=M-2^{5k+1}N,\qquad
C=M+2^{5k+1}N.
\]

Walsh orthogonality gives \(|\Phi|\le1\), hence \(|N|\le M\). Therefore
\[
|A|,\ |B|,\ |C|
\le (2^{5k+1}+1)M
<2^{w-1}.
\]

Their residues lie in the range where the high bit correctly identifies the sign of the ordinary integer. Thus
\[
p_1=[A\ge0],\qquad
p_0=[B\ge0]\land[C\ge0]
\]
are exactly the required comparisons.

This proves the endpoint using the invariant the implementation actually maintains. The ideal intermediate-integer bounds are useful mathematical facts, but they do not automatically identify every stored word with an unrestricted signed intermediate integer. The rewritten section keeps those claims separate.
