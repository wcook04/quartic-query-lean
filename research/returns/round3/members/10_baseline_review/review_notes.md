At (k=3,\ell=6), substituting the advertised depth scale makes one nonnegative term grow like (sqrt{\log(3m)}) while the claimed replacement stays bounded. This shows failure of the displayed comparison between sums, not only failure of a termwise derivation. The optional weak-log deduction retains the actual exponent and completes amplification, off-promise acceptance estimates, geometric summation and the eventual comparison with (L). **Stein explanation.** The selected three-block version succeeded in accounting for every chain-rule contribution without extra notation beyond (D_{r,a}). The alternative two-block draft was not also inserted; duplicating both would add length without explaining the second elimination's independence issue. The retained degree-three example serves a different purpose and includes its symmetry qualification.

**Order.** Moving §§3.7–3.16 would remove approximately 5195 of 11160 whitespace tokens before the total construction. This is a reading-path measurement, not a proof simplification claim. Every proof and anchor must remain. The move depends on preserving the source caveat near the main interface and updating links after relettering.

Current exact types also reveal real residual differences: success-reflection access, generic compiler count, full-state versus measured-law equality, fixed candidate/address workspace, and the optional (m+1) estimate. These distinctions should remain local rather than be hidden inside a blanket “formalized” label.
