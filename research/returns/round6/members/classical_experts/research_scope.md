The substitution gives a sufficient factor-two-weaker tail for the actual r=40t construction. It does not establish the exact fair intermediate bound for arbitrary r, and it does not apply the same substitution to the illustrative r=2 toy. The fair lemma is retained as correct and useful. See direct_product_comparison.md.

**Predecessor variants (CX002).** The near-quartic D–Q back-pointer family is separated from the Monte-Carlo-hard no-back-pointer balanced-tree variant. No claim says that all pointer functions are easy for randomized algorithms. The binary-field observation concerns selective access and delayed verification, not binary encoding alone.

The record’s criticism of a displayed logarithmic comparison was verified against supplied Bansal–Sinha arXiv v3, printed p. 38 only. It does not disprove their theorem, establish an erratum, or assert the same issue in the separately supplied proceedings version. The external SSW Fourier transfer is distinguished from transfer of its random-matrix hard distribution.

It identifies inconsistent prose by comparing the three documents with the supplied independent AI statement-fidelity report and the visible commit pins. No new build, comparison or full semantic proof audit was performed. An inconsistency in documentation is not a claim that the recorded formal proof fails.
