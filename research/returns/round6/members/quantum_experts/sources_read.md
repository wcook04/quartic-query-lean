## How the source introductions actually allocate attention

The sources do not support a universal rule that an introduction must be short. DH is a two-page note that exposes its task and algorithm immediately; HMW foregrounds a precise simulation obstacle; MPS gives a quantum overview before the full proof; AIK introduces the candidate-set invariant before its detailed estimates. BDK reuses the stages of one concrete construction for randomized and quantum analyses. AA, BS, Tal and SSW have substantial introductions/technical overviews. What transfers is the ordering of question, interface, obstacle and mechanism—not a fixed number of pages. These observations come from the named passages above, not author interviews.
