## What the numbers measure

Current space is estimated from physical PDF section boundaries, using `page - 1 + heading_y / page_height`. The first interval includes the title; the last includes references and trailing white space. Float placement and margins are charged to the containing interval. These are page-equivalents, not word shares or measured reading times; allow about 0.1 page of interpretive uncertainty. Percentages use all 14 or 125 physical pages, so titles and references remain in the denominator. Desired shares are panel judgments, not empirical learning measurements.

`evidence/page_measurements.json` contains the measured boundaries. The source-status file describes the record PDF as belonging to an earlier accepted source, although its visible opening already contains the reading guide. Accordingly its page shares below are measurements of the supplied PDF only; they do not certify a fresh render of the current Markdown. All findings quote current Markdown.
