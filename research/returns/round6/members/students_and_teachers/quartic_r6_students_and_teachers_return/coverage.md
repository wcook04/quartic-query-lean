# Coverage and the role of each element

The combined review read all current exposition and expert-companion Markdown, and selected reading routes and proof destinations in the long record. All 14 pages of the supplied exposition PDF were visually inspected; the expert and record openings were visually sampled. The supplied PDFs were rasterized for inspection, not newly typeset. The 125-page record was **not** read or visually audited page by page.

Source locators below are paths within the input packet. They do not imply that the source manuscripts are included again in this return. The exact quotations and fuller reasons are in `findings.json` and `findings.md`; the SX identifiers below refer to those final findings.

**Keep** preserves an element's purpose. **Deepen** supplies the missing connection or reader action. **Clarify** repairs scope, prerequisites or navigation. **Move** means offer existing detail on an optional route, while keeping the necessary statement on the main route. These are recommendations only: no manuscript was rewritten.
