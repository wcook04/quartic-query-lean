# Reader-principle audit

This maps all fourteen criteria in `reader_principles.md` in the original packet to the current 43-finding review. “Met” refers to the inspected presentation, not proof correctness or demonstrated learning. “Partial” identifies a useful feature with a remaining specific obstacle. “Route-dependent” means the judgment changes between a first reading, a selected proof excursion and a full audit. No actual student or lecturer took part in a reader trial.
