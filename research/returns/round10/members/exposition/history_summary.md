## Custody, schema and limits

No archived reviewer program was executed. These checks establish custody, not that every archived file or every original finding was semantically reviewed.

The cross-document checks are limited to the listed current local passages. They do not establish a new Lean compilation, a repository-wide proof audit, an independent record-PDF review or fresh remote verification of every pinned link. The 353 uninspected current R9 IDs are an explicit limit, rather than a claim that the entire three-panel history has been closed.
