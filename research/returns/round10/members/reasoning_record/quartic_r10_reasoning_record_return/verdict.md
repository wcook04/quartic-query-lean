## What would make the next edition excellent

A merely serviceable revision would add labels and links while leaving the same long interruptions in the complete route. A stronger edition would let the reader understand the object before the most technical supplier proof, identify a whole algorithm before its subroutine analysis, and carry one probability question through each reduction. Detailed alternatives would remain recoverable, but each would have a defined purpose and an appropriate position.

The exact count should be reproducible, not just plausible. The running example should retain its identity across operations. Source-status statements should say exactly what was checked. A formal-evidence reader should be able to answer what the current statement is, what its hypotheses mean, which route establishes it and what historical execution was recorded without first reconciling a chronology.
