# Writing method for the quartic papers

The three accounts use the existing methods in
[`wcook04/lean-exposition`](https://github.com/wcook04/lean-exposition):
`graph-to-long` for the reasoning record, `long-to-short` for the expert
companion, and `record-to-expository` for the teaching article. The register
stage reviews every paragraph after those transitions. Round seven starts
from upstream `5a9a3ab` (including writing-method changes #40–42) and refines
those owners on `codex/quartic-r7-writing-method`. The
[method revision](../research/adjudication/round7_method_revision.md) records
the exact base, changed owners, source readings, transfer trials, finding
dispositions and validation; it is the provenance for this application.

The expert reader already knows the query models and standard search tools.
The companion therefore concentrates on unrestricted losing comparisons,
the conditional sampler and the conversion of a cell query into an address
prediction with a fixed cap. The exposition has a different job: readers
without query-complexity training must be able to perform the operations used
at those transitions. A new prerequisite list does not repair that audience
mismatch. The record supplies complete derivations and separate destinations
for construction, classical lower bound, Forrelation and statement auditing.

Open with the mathematical task, the result's scope and its decisive mechanism.
Give credit where a result or construction enters. Introduce detailed historical
parameter calculations and refined constants when the objects make them useful.
In the exposition, work an actual small transition before requiring the general
operation; retain a familiar supplier's contract and send optional numerical
derivations to their exact record destination. Expansion is justified by a
missing inference, and relocation by a change in reader task. Neither length
nor the number of revised sentences measures success.

Every paragraph receives attention, with deliberate retention an acceptable
decision. Recheck the meaning of probability events, time-indexed histories,
quantifier order, fixed caps and realised actions after editing prose as well
as formulas. A literal figure must match its stated data. A linked proof must
contain the promised inference, and its visible label and return instruction
must match the current organisation. The method dispositions distinguish new
refinements from existing instructions reapplied and local choices that should
not become general laws.

Calibrate prose with inspected passages from cited research papers and teaching
sources. Choose comparisons by genre and task, inspect extraction, and treat
register statistics as pointers to paragraphs. Preserve mathematically useful
negation, genuine parallel objects and accurate attribution when a detector
flags them. Punctuation preferences and movement toward a corpus median do
not establish understanding. Render and inspect every delivered page after
the substantive revision, then attempt the claimed reading tasks. Simulated
reader reconstruction is editorial evidence, separate from observed human
learning.

Report formalisation with readable links and a precise statement of any
material mathematical difference. Put declaration-level comparisons, ordinary
transport, execution receipts and their limits in the linked verification reference.
An endpoint correspondence does not certify every step of the printed proof.
The [statement evidence contract](statement_evidence_contract.md) governs those
claims; a one-page appendix quota does not.

The linked verification reference is itself a reading surface. Use full-width
scholarly paragraphs for explanations of hypotheses, transport and checking
scope. Keep a table when its entries are compact, genuinely comparative and
readable at the final size. Inspect wrapping and density, not only whether
the material fits inside the page. Rendered tables that passed a no-overflow
check can still fail that reading test.

Use the existing reader-name index in `lean_links.json`, produced by
`tools/build_lean_links.py`, for natural-language links to formal statements.
The index retains the exact symbol, source path, revision and line span behind
the link; ordinary prose need not expose them as a long identifier. The same
route applies to the record as to the companions. A raw-identifier paragraph
is not an adequate repair for a cramped table. The round-seven correction
reopens the rejected G.4 acceptance and requires the receiving editor to
inspect the repaired pages and link destinations.

## Current reading-edition boundary

The papers carry the mathematics. The proof index carries natural-language summaries and links to substantive formal proofs; machine-readable mappings retain identifiers, paths, versions and checks. Historical verification material lives in its own reference, not in printed appendices added by default.

Apply reviewer requests for traceability at the appropriate destination. A request for exact theorem names, source hashes or run history is usually a request to improve the linked index. Keep only the mathematical model, theorem scope and genuine correspondence limitation in the paper. Check actual rendered text for leaked filenames, hashes, declaration names and workflow labels; clickable destinations can preserve them without printing them. A source-only index or a wrapper ending in a placeholder statement does not answer a reader looking for the proof: provide both the specification and the substantive implementation, labelled by role.

This corrects a failure to apply the existing upstream rule, rather than inventing a new writing principle. The expert paper and long account are two applications; a reproducibility or systems paper is an exception when the checking process itself is the subject.

## Round-eight refinements

The [round-eight dispositions](../research/adjudication/round8_synthesis.json)
separate preserved strengths, applied changes and suggestions not adopted. The
original [returns](../research/returns/round8/README.md) retain every reader's
perspective and qualifications. They are simulated reviews, not a human study.
The lecturer's answer-exposed recomputation is useful for checking answers but
is not a cold attempt. No fresh reader-learning result is inferred from revision.

At each example, identify the input, legal domain, required output and charged
resource. A unique-mark search outputs an index; deciding OR under that same
promise is constant. Name changes of board or supplier when they occur, then
reuse those names. Put detailed differences between a teaching example and a
finite checker at the checker's landing passage, with a short local scope notice.

Test a difficult operation by reconstructing its state, transformation, retained
data, inverse and measured output. For this revision, the two-pivot state and
reflection conjugation make those operations visible. A nearby changed case
should test the repair: the second fractional pivot tests multiplication into
existing weights, a second Grover iteration tests overshoot, and a later target
query tests whole-run lightness. Keep the solutions separate. Optional reading
routes carry explicit conclusions to their rejoin point and have appropriate
questions; the combined teach-back requires both routes.

A law-preserving contract must name the procedure, conditioning event and output.
A reusable reduction must state its finite objects, circuit semantics, inherited
parameters and caps locally. Preserve the parameter order in navigation too:
finish the sufficient fixed-parameter proof before an optional uniform refinement.
Attribute the inherited theorem, reconstructed constants and borrowed analytic
ingredients separately, without inferring independent priority.

For each proof link, identify its task: statement, derivation, worked execution,
source declaration or audit. Open the delivered edition at the anchor and check
that the reader can perform the promised task from there. A constant envelope
needs its derivation, not just an earlier count; a simulator example needs its
trace, not just its definition. Record the edition behind readable link text.

Use a genre-matched, edition-bound subset for any numerical register comparison,
and record extraction checks before trusting its numbers. Whole-document counts
and a broad literature shelf have different roles. This revision makes no new
numerical register comparison. Author lists, necessary case splits, quantifiers
and negated false implications are functional prose, not automatic style faults.
Paragraph boundaries follow changes of mathematical task; approximate length
heuristics do not override a complete contract or induction step.

Inspect page transitions as well as isolated figures. Give each figure one
reading operation, check it at final size, and move long explanations to the
caption rather than shrinking the labels. Preserve counterexamples according to
the mistaken inference they prevent. Record deliberate retention as a decision,
not as an unperformed edit or a claim that every reviewer suggestion was adopted.

When renumbering a local lemma, update its internal references separately from
locators in cited sources; a new local number never renumbers the source theorem.

## Chronological first-encounter review

For round nine, review the reader's accumulating understanding in publication
order, sentence by sentence. Before proceeding, record what the earlier text
makes available, the new inference or prerequisite, the unresolved question and
the expected next step. Later knowledge cannot erase an earlier difficulty;
append its resolving location. Log searches, link detours and answer exposure.
This applies to displays, captions, tables, footnotes and appendices as well as
prose. It supplements proof checking and thematic editing, which can otherwise
hide dependencies by assembling information from distant sections.

Freeze the first-pass log before reading old reviews or checking the literature.
Then verify proposed criticisms against the current text and sources. Require a
specific reader cost and repair direction, not praise, obligatory balance or a
repeated complaint counted several times. Keep sentence-level coverage separate
from improvement findings; an empty issue list is not a perfection verdict.
A numerical target is a prompt for thoroughness, never a reason to fabricate a
fault. Declare unreviewed ranges rather than claiming completeness from volume.

An AI model's chronological audit is a simulation, not an observation of a new
human reader. It must disclose prior familiarity and cannot create independent
blind readers by adopting several perspectives in the same context. This is a
new review procedure to test, not evidence that reader comprehension improved.
The [round-nine protocol](../research/requests/round9/chronological_reading.md)
defines the reading-state and coverage records used to assess it.

## Round-nine application and refinement

The [round-nine manuscript decisions](../research/adjudication/round9_revision.md)
apply this method separately to the expert companion, the teaching article and
the complete record. The [48 method decisions and transfer cases](../research/adjudication/round9_method_revision.json)
bind the reusable changes to `lean-exposition` commit
`7bc9ef5d253d23527c6ccbd759c2eb24c0243ac7`, merged in [pull request 44](https://github.com/wcook04/lean-exposition/pull/44), in its existing authoring guide,
reader-review guide and three stage skills. Many findings require an existing
rule to be applied more carefully; they do not justify another parallel guide.

Before accepting a claim that an explanation is missing, read the preceding
definition, the whole paragraph and its continuation. Distinguish absence from
late resolution and from a preference for a different presentation. Group
repairs by the inference they restore, keep individual dispositions, and reread
from immediately before each repair through its next use. A preview that moves
a formula earlier must not introduce unexplained notation of its own.

At a change of parameter regime or algorithm model, state what is fixed and
what changes, then give the connecting substitution or averaging step. The
expert paper's growing-parameter family replaces the fixed-parameter allowance;
the randomized argument averages deterministic trees under one unchanged cap.
In an operational description, give an admissible parameter choice and every
exit's output contract. An asymptotic cost estimate alone does not define the
bounded algorithm. Retained workspace is sufficient when the consuming proof
uses its output law; do not impose a clean-output operation that the proof does
not need.

These choices have specific literary precedents. Knuth, Larrabee and Roberts,
[*Mathematical Writing*](https://www-cs-faculty.stanford.edu/~knuth/papers/cs1193.pdf),
§1 items 5 and 10–14 (printed pp. 2–3), connect self-contained statements,
first-use definitions and the reader's available knowledge. Their §10 (pp.
20–21) motivates visible algorithm steps and invariants without prescribing a
named style. Francis Su's
[*Guidelines for Good Mathematical Writing*](https://math.hmc.edu/su/wp-content/uploads/sites/10/2020/08/Guidelines-for-Good-Mathematical-Writing.pdf),
18 August 2020, pp. 1–3, distinguishes useful omitted algebra from assumptions
and explanations the intended reader needs. We use that distinction to repair
inferential gaps while retaining concise routine calculations. Childs's
[*Lecture Notes on Quantum Algorithms*](https://www.cs.umd.edu/~amchilds/qa/qa.pdf),
17 April 2025, §1.4, distinguishes retained intermediate values from clean output
through their actual maps. That example informs the exposition's account of
retained registers and rejects an unnecessary cleaning requirement. The
manuscript revision notes record further inspected, genre-matched passages.
These readings guide composition; they are neither proofs of our mathematical
claims nor measurements of learning.

Keep a review's frozen encounter log separate from retrospective annotations,
source-assisted corrections and later render inspection. Preserve the real
order and any exposure to solutions or earlier feedback. Current-status-first
formal appendices should identify the checked object and evidence boundary,
then distinguish historical states. Their technical vocabulary serves a
separate task from the main mathematical argument.

Finally, test links from the distributed artifact's location. The packet builder
now retains the source writing guide at its repository path and rebases the
compatibility copy's links, including their packaged dependency closure. A link
that works beside the source may fail after relocation; the original round-nine
packets remain immutable evidence of what was actually sent.

## Round-ten expert-return application

The first R10 return contributes 15 manuscript findings and eight method
observations. The [method dispositions](../research/adjudication/round10_method_revision.json)
apply them to the existing public authoring, reader-review and long-to-short
owners. The [revision account](../research/adjudication/round10_revision.md)
records the first-return manuscript changes; the later two returns are discussed below.

This pass tests the default reading order against the suggested detours: keep a
complete finite contract, apply it to the construction-specific quantity, then
supply the generic implementation at a precise destination. The exact event,
law and cap must survive that move. The record comparison uses matched supplier
scales, and its counterexample states which inference it refutes. Formula scope
is checked across rendered floats, not only adjacent source lines. These are
applications of existing rules, not measured gains in reader understanding.

The return's first pass exposed bounded blocks of sentences. Its frozen record
is preserved with that limit; it is not relabelled as isolated-unit reading.
For a future strict pass, the public review procedure now requires persisting
each encounter before revealing the next semantic unit, including generated
references at their delivered position.

## Follow-up: one review, two audiences

The [informal expert assessment](../research/returns/round10/followup_editorial_feedback.md)
asks for a clearer contribution, motivation before technical machinery, and
visible identification of external appendices. Those are already requirements
of the public transition skills. This pass repairs their application: inspect
the opening as a continuous reading experience and read references without
following their hyperlinks. The assessment's scores and suggested roadmap length
are editorial advice, not acceptance thresholds.

Its transfer to the exposition is a separate authoring decision. The expert
reader needs the decisive mechanism and complete contracts; the learner needs
the worked operations that make those contracts usable. Preserve the latter
when reducing the former's implementation detail. The [method record](../research/adjudication/round10_followup_method.json)
tests this distinction on the SK papers and on the architectural companion and
consumer guide. The existing public authoring guide now checks the reading task
and retained content separately for each receiving account. These are editorial
checks, not a new review of the exposition or measured gains in understanding.

## Complete R10 reading routes

The [remaining review decisions](../research/adjudication/round10_remaining_dispositions.json)
apply the record and exposition returns and the second informal expert
assessment. The [14 method dispositions](../research/adjudication/round10_remaining_method.json)
mostly require existing rules to be applied: preserve whole algorithm contracts,
exact conditional laws and all-input caps while improving order; distinguish a
worked example from the actual input checked by a linked program; and audit
constructor summands before printing an exact count.

One refinement belongs in the existing public authoring guide: assess the
complete reading route separately from entry at a particular proof step. A
usable shortcut can coexist with a costly default order. Before moving a long
derivation, list the contracts used before their proofs and the destinations
that discharge them. An existing interface defeats an absence claim without
necessarily settling a sequencing concern. This is the change merged at `6c7226e4c5512795c381a661a9c2777f4a2ef1cd` in
[pull request 46](https://github.com/wcook04/lean-exposition/pull/46), tested against
the SK argument and the architecture companion's executable consumer route.
The method record retains those source-grounded transfer checks and their limits.

Neither prospective batching nor retrospective interpretation is relabelled as
an isolated-sentence first reading. A later correction supplies a current
locator without rewriting a frozen log. A construction-first order is useful
here; it is not a universal rule for specialist papers or historical accounts.

and classical complexity measures remain explicit. Existing public authoring

records application without claiming a new general rule or measured learning gain.
