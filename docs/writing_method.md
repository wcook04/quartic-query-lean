# Writing method for the quartic papers

The expert companion (`expert_companion.md`) follows `long-to-short`, and the exposition (`exposition.md`) follows `record-to-expository`, in `wcook04/lean-exposition` at commit `b05c31c` (`skills/`). The quartic case is recorded there as a worked instance in `skills/README.md`.

1. Model the reader first. The readers are the authors of the query-complexity papers we cite. List what their papers already contain, which we cite and do not re-derive, and what this result asks them to learn.
2. Put the one point on page one: R ≈ m·L with L ≈ m^(1−1/k), against Q ≈ √m. Add the history 5/2, 8/3, 3, now 4, with correct credit to cheat sheets, pointer functions and Forrelation.
3. Link Lean as plain words to pinned line ranges, with no dotted identifiers in prose. End the companion with the one-page "Checking the statement" appendix keyed to `lean/QuarticCorpus/Unconditional/Challenge.lean` and its four compared theorems.
4. Write formal status that is still being rebuilt conditionally. Leave out provenance debris and house vocabulary. Use no em dashes, contrasts or taglines.
5. Render the papers and look at every page. Revision passes refine existing text and do not expand it. Fold reader feedback back through `skills/README.md`, "Where reader feedback goes".
